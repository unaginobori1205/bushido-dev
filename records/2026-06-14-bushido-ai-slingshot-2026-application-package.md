## ② One-line description（エレベーター）

> **BUSHIDO AI is the cultural intelligence platform that uses AI to match global travelers, schools, and premium clients with authentic, verified Japanese cultural experiences — and orchestrates masters, interpreters, travel, and itinerary into one trusted layer.**

---

## ③ Problem（課題）

> Japan welcomed a record number of inbound visitors, yet most travelers only access surface-level sightseeing. The experiences that make Japan unique — kyudo, tea ceremony, calligraphy, Zen, shrine culture, and the living wisdom of local masters — remain fragmented, hard to book, and locked behind language and cultural barriers.
>
> At the same time, the local masters and regional providers who hold this value are weak at English, online booking, payment, and quality assurance. The result: travelers cannot find authentic experiences, and the people who could deliver them cannot reach the global market.

---

## ④ Solution（解決策）

> BUSHIDO AI uses AI to personalize cultural journeys based on each visitor's interests, purpose, budget, location, and learning goals. We combine verified local masters, interpreters, premium transportation, itinerary design, and cultural education into one trusted experience layer — a "cultural experience OS" for Japan.
>
> We are not a tour agency. We are building the infrastructure that distributes the non-digital value of Japanese culture to the world, powered by AI and a trust-based local network.

---

## ⑤ Technology & Innovation（ディープテック要素 — 重要）

> - **AI matching & personalization engine** — maps traveler intent (purpose, cultural interest, budget, location, language, learning goal) to the optimal combination of master, experience, interpreter, transport, and lodging.
> - **Trust & verification layer** — a curated, verified network of authentic masters with quality, language, and reliability data; trust-based community data that pure marketplaces cannot replicate.
> - **Cross-language cultural translation** — AI bridges not just words but cultural context, so meaning (not just language) is conveyed.
> - **Itinerary orchestration** — auto-assembles end-to-end journeys (experience + interpreter + transport + lodging + learning).
>
> **Moat:** the combination of AI + a real, founder-built trust network. The trust cannot be reproduced by AI alone or by a generic OTA.

## ⑥ Business Model（収益）

> 1. Experience commission — fee on each booked cultural experience.
> 2. Premium / VIP tours — high-value curated journeys for HNW clients.
> 3. Education programs — cultural programs for schools and universities.
> 4. Corporate training — bushido / Japanese-culture programs for companies.
> 5. Listing / subscription — monthly fee for masters and regional providers.

---

## ⑦ Market（市場）

> Japan's inbound tourism is at an all-time high. In 2025, Japan welcomed a record **42.7 million** international visitors — the first time ever above 40 million (+15.8% YoY) — and visitor spending hit a record **¥9.5 trillion** (+16.4% YoY, ~¥229,000 per visitor). The government targets **60 million visitors and ¥15 trillion in spending by 2030**, with an explicit shift from quantity to quality: 40 million repeat visitors and 130 million overnight stays in regional areas.
>
> This national pivot toward high-value, repeat, regional, experience-driven tourism is exactly the wave BUSHIDO AI rides. HNW and experience-seeking travelers — especially from Singapore and ASEAN — are the fastest-growing, highest-margin segment, and they want authentic, in-depth, personalized experiences, not mass tourism.
>
> Beachhead: inbound travelers, schools, and corporates from Singapore & ASEAN.
> Expansion: all of Japan, then Japanese-culture programs delivered into ASEAN markets.
>
> Source: JNTO / Japan Tourism Agency (JTA), 2025 figures; "Tourism Nation Promotion Basic Plan" 2030 targets.

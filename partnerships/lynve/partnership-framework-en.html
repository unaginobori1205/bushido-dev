BUSHIDO JAPAN  ×  LYNVÉ

Partnership Framework

Private cultural experiences between Switzerland and Japan · Draft for discussion

Prepared in response to your message of 29 June 2026. This document answers the six points you raised and proposes a pilot-first collaboration. All figures below are a proposed starting point, open to discussion on our mid-July call.

Guiding principle

Clear, professional, and mutually beneficial from the very start. Lynvé owns the client relationship in Europe; BUSHIDO JAPAN is your trusted operator on the ground in Japan.

1. Partnership structure

  • A non-exclusive partnership between Lynvé (Switzerland / Europe) and BUSHIDO JAPAN (Japan).
  • Lynvé — client-facing partner in Europe: sources clients, owns the client relationship, and sets the final retail price.
  • BUSHIDO JAPAN — ground operator (DMC) in Japan: designs and delivers the experience, and manages masters, venues, dining, vehicles, guiding, and on-site operations.
  • Phase 1 is a pilot (see below), followed by a simple written Partnership Agreement.

2. Revenue-sharing model

Proposed: Net-rate model. BUSHIDO provides Lynvé a confidential net rate; Lynvé adds her margin and sets the client price, keeping full control of pricing and the client relationship. Suggested Lynvé margin: around 20% (net rate = retail − 20%).

A referral-commission model (BUSHIDO invoices the client and pays Lynvé a commission) is also possible if you prefer — happy to discuss. Flights, main hotels, and third-party bookings Lynvé arranges herself are outside this scope.

3. Payment process

  • Currency: JPY (EUR equivalent shown for reference).
  • Deposit: 50% on booking confirmation, to secure the private masters, venues (e.g. the Nagoya Castle charter), and vehicles.
  • Balance: 100% no later than 30 days before the experience date.
  • Method: international bank transfer or Wise.

4. Roles & client responsibilities

  • Lynvé provides: client booking & briefing, guest preferences (dietary, medical, mobility), confirmation that guests hold travel insurance, and flights / main accommodation unless BUSHIDO is asked to arrange them.
  • BUSHIDO provides: itinerary design, all local bookings (masters, venues, restaurants, private vehicle), professional guiding, on-the-ground operations, safety, and a local support contact throughout the experience.

5. Cancellation terms

Because private masters and venues are reserved in advance, a tiered policy applies (proposed):

Notice before experienceCharge
60+ daysDeposit only (non-refundable)
30–59 days50% of total
15–29 days75% of total
0–14 days or no-show100% of total

Certain experiences (the private Nagoya Castle charter, specific masters) may carry stricter supplier terms, which we will flag at the time of booking. In cases of force majeure (disaster, illness, travel bans), we offer rescheduling or credit rather than forfeiture.

6. Customer communication

  • You remain the client's primary point of contact.
  • During planning, BUSHIDO communicates with and through you; on the ground, BUSHIDO supports the guest directly while keeping you fully informed.
  • Non-circumvention: BUSHIDO will not directly solicit or take on your introduced clients outside this partnership. Mutual confidentiality applies — your clients remain yours.

7. Proposed pilot collaboration

  • Product: Nagoya 1-Day VIP Cultural Experience — Kyudo, tea ceremony, Atsuta Shrine, a private Nagoya Castle tour, and refined local dining. Extendable to Kyoto or Ise.
  • Scale: 1–2 pilot bookings, target autumn 2026.
  • Terms: a simplified version of the above; full agreement after a joint debrief.
  • Goal: test the process, client experience, and working style before a long-term agreement.

8. Next step

  • A video call in mid-July 2026 to align on the above and confirm the pilot.
  • After alignment, BUSHIDO issues a one-page pilot agreement and a sample net-rate sheet for the Nagoya experience.

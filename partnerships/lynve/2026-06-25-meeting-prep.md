Bring these as *options*, not demands. These are starting points — negotiate, don't fix live.

| Model | How it works | Good when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Referral / introduction fee** | Lyn refers a client; BUSHIDO delivers; Lyn earns a referral % (luxury norm ≈ 10–15%). | Simple, low-commitment start. |

open to C. → まずは1商品で軽く始め、Cへ発展させる。

## 7. Trust-building strategy / 信頼関係の築き方
- **Listen first.** Let her talk about her "why" before you pitch. / まず聞く。
- **Lead with values, not specs.** *"Transformation, not tourism."* / 価値観で語る。
- **Show, don't tell.** The model plan proves capability quietly. / 見せて示す。
- **Don't oversell.** Stance: *"We can realize your world on the Japan side."* / 売り込まない。
- **Be reliable on the small things.** Send the promised follow-up *same day*. / 約束を守る。
- **Protect the essence.** Mention you won't cheapen traditions — partners (e.g.
  Ogasawara-ryu) value this; so will discerning clients. / 本質を守る姿勢。
- **Think long-term.** This is a relationship to *grow*, not a deal to close. / 長く育てる縁。

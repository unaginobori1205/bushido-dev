## 0. Guiding principle / 基本姿勢
Clear, professional, and **mutually beneficial from the start** — exactly as Lyn asked.
Lynvé owns the client relationship in Europe; BUSHIDO JAPAN is the trusted operator on
the ground in Japan. 明確・プロフェッショナル・双方に利益。顧客関係はLynvéが保持、日本側の実行はBUSHIDO。

---

## 1. Partnership structure / 提携の構造
- **Non-exclusive partnership** between Lynvé (Switzerland/Europe) and BUSHIDO JAPAN (Japan).
- **Roles / 役割分担**:
  - **Lynvé** — client-facing partner in Europe: sources clients, owns the client
    relationship, sets the final retail price. / 顧客獲得・顧客関係・最終価格の決定。
  - **BUSHIDO JAPAN** — ground operator (DMC) in Japan: designs and delivers the
    experience, manages masters, venues, dining, vehicles, guiding, and on-site
    operations. / 企画・手配・当日運営・現地サポート。
- **Phase 1 = Pilot** (see §7), then a simple written **Partnership Agreement** for
  ongoing work. / まずパイロット→簡易な提携契約書へ。

## 2. Revenue-sharing model / 収益分配モデル
Two clean options — **we propose Option A**:

- **Option A — Net-rate (recommended) / ネットレート方式（推奨）**
  BUSHIDO provides Lynvé a confidential **net rate**; Lynvé adds her margin and sets the
  client price. Lynvé keeps full control of pricing and the client relationship.
  *Proposed Lynvé margin: ~20% (i.e. net rate = retail − 20%).*
  BUSHIDOが卸値を提示、Lynvéがマージンを乗せて顧客価格を決定。**目安マージン20%**。

- **Option B — Referral commission / 紹介手数料方式**
  BUSHIDO invoices the client directly and pays Lynvé a **referral commission**.
  *Proposed commission: ~15% of the experience value.*
  BUSHIDOが顧客に直接請求、Lynvéに**紹介料15%**を支払う。

> Flights, main hotels, and third-party bookings Lynvé arranges herself are outside this
> scope. 航空券・主要ホテル等はスコープ外。

## 3. Payment process / 支払いプロセス
- **Currency / 通貨**: JPY (EUR equivalent shown for reference). / 円建て（EUR併記）。
- **Deposit / デポジット**: **50%** on booking confirmation, to secure masters, private
  venues (e.g. Nagoya Castle charter), and vehicles. / 手配確保のため確定時に**50%**。
- **Balance / 残金**: **100% no later than 30 days** before the experience date. / 体験の**30日前**までに残金。
- **Method / 方法**: international bank transfer or Wise. / 国際送金またはWise。
- Under Option A, Lynvé collects from the client and settles the net rate with BUSHIDO on
  this schedule; under Option B, BUSHIDO collects and remits Lynvé's commission within
  14 days after the experience. / A案はLynが集金しネット精算、B案は体験後14日以内に紹介料支払い。

## 4. Roles & client responsibilities / 顧客側の責任範囲
- **Lynvé provides / Lynvé側**: client booking & briefing, guest preferences (dietary,
  medical, mobility), confirmation that guests hold **travel insurance**, and flights /
  main accommodation unless BUSHIDO is asked to arrange them. / 予約・要望共有・旅行保険確認・航空券等。
- **BUSHIDO provides / BUSHIDO側**: itinerary design, all local bookings (masters,
  venues, restaurants, private vehicle), professional guiding, on-the-ground operations,
  safety, and a local support contact during the experience. / 企画・現地手配・運営・安全・当日サポート。

## 5. Cancellation terms / キャンセル規定
Because private masters and venues are reserved in advance, a tiered policy applies
(proposed). 師匠・貸切会場を事前確保するため、段階的な規定を提案:

| Notice before experience / 事前告知 | Charge / charge |
| --- | --- |
| 60+ days / 60日以上前 | Deposit only (non-refundable) / デポジットのみ |
| 30–59 days / 30〜59日前 | 50% of total / 総額の50% |
| 15–29 days / 15〜29日前 | 75% of total / 総額の75% |
| 0–14 days or no-show / 14日以内・不参加 | 100% of total / 総額の100% |

- Certain experiences (private Nagoya Castle charter, specific masters) may carry
  stricter supplier terms, flagged at booking. / 一部体験はより厳しい規定あり（予約時に明示）。
- **Force majeure / 不可抗力**: for disasters, illness, or travel bans, we offer
  **rescheduling or credit** rather than forfeiture. / 災害・疾病等は振替またはクレジット対応。

## 6. Customer communication / 顧客コミュニケーションの管理
- **Lynvé remains the client's primary point of contact.** / 顧客の主担当はLynvéが保持。
- During planning, BUSHIDO communicates **with and through Lynvé**; on the ground,
  BUSHIDO supports the guest directly while keeping Lynvé informed. / 企画中はLyn経由、現地は直接対応＋共有。
- **Non-circumvention / 顧客横取り禁止**: BUSHIDO will not directly solicit or take on
  Lynvé's introduced clients outside this partnership. Mutual confidentiality applies.
  BUSHIDOはLynvéの顧客を個別に勧誘しない。相互に守秘。
  *(This protects Lynvé's business and is offered proactively to build trust.)*

- **Goal / 目的**: test process, client experience, and working style before a long-term

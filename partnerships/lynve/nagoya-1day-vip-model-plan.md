## Concept / コンセプト

**"A day to meet the Japanese spirit — not as a tourist, but as a guest."**
**「観光ではなく、日本の精神に出会う一日」**

Nagoya is the birthplace of the samurai world. The three men who unified Japan —
**Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi, and Tokugawa Ieyasu (the "Three Heroes")** —
all came from this region. It holds Atsuta Shrine, one of Japan's three most sacred
shrines, and Nagoya Castle, seat of the Tokugawa. Yet it is far less crowded than
Tokyo and Kyoto — perfect for a private, refined, quiet-luxury journey.

名古屋は武家文化発祥の地。三英傑（信長・秀吉・家康）の地であり、熱田神宮と名古屋城を擁する。
東京・京都ほど混雑せず、プライベートで静かな上質体験に最適。

**Why this fits Lynvé's clients**: wellness & mindfulness (Kyudo, Zen, tea),
quiet luxury (private access, no crowds), cultural depth (real masters, real
traditions), and longevity / inner calm (a day designed to align body and mind).

## 3. Samurai Heritage — the region / 武家文化（地域全体）
This region is an open-air museum of samurai history. Beyond Nagoya Castle:

- **Nagoya Castle / 名古屋城** — seat of the Owari-Tokugawa. Its reconstructed
  **Hommaru Palace (本丸御殿)** was completed in 1615 as the lord's residence and
  later became the **exclusive lodging of the Tokugawa Shogun**. Rebuilt over 10
  years (¥15B), 30+ rooms of gold and cypress — a national special historic site.
- **Inuyama Castle / 犬山城** — one of Japan's oldest original keeps (national treasure).
- **Okazaki Castle / 岡崎城** — birthplace of Tokugawa Ieyasu.
- **Kiyosu Castle / 清洲城** — Nobunaga's early stronghold.
- **Gifu Castle / 岐阜城** — Nobunaga's mountaintop castle, "the road to unifying Japan."
- **Shogun-linked tea rooms / 将軍ゆかりの茶室** — for an authentic, historic tea setting.

**★ Private charter tour of Nagoya Castle / 名古屋城の貸切ツアー**
Through BUSHIDO JAPAN's direct connection with **Nagoya City**, a **private
chartered tour of Nagoya Castle is possible — for groups of up to 8 guests.**
A rare, exclusive access that ordinary tours cannot offer.
名古屋市とのつながりにより、**8名まで**名古屋城の貸切ツアーが可能。通常では得られない特別なアクセス。

## 5. Local Cuisine / 地元の食
Nagoya has a bold, beloved food culture — and BUSHIDO JAPAN knows the people
behind it. **The owners and chefs are friends, seniors, and juniors** of the
founder, opening doors to private rooms and special arrangements.

名古屋の食文化を、その担い手とのつながりで案内。**店主・社長は友人や先輩後輩**。
個室や特別対応の扉が開く。

- **Live tuna-cutting show / マグロ解体ショー** — theatrical and delicious.

- **Miso-nikomi udon / 味噌煮込みうどん** — rich red-miso comfort food.
- **Miso-katsu / みそかつ** — the iconic Nagoya pork cutlet.
- **Misen / 味仙** — legendary Taiwan ramen, Nagoya soul food.
- **Wakashachiya / 若鯱家** — famous curry udon.

→ From refined private *kaiseki* to beloved local soul food — calibrated to the guest.

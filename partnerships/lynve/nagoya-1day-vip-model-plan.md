## Concept / コンセプト

**"A day to meet the Japanese spirit — not as a tourist, but as a guest."**
**「観光ではなく、日本の精神に出会う一日」**

Nagoya is the heart of samurai history (Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi,
Tokugawa Ieyasu were all born nearby) and home to Atsuta Shrine, one of Japan's
three most sacred shrines. Yet it is uncrowded compared to Tokyo and Kyoto —
which makes it perfect for a private, refined, quiet-luxury experience.

名古屋は三英傑の地であり、熱田神宮を擁する。東京・京都ほど混雑せず、
プライベートで静かな上質体験に最適。

**Why this works for Lynvé's clients:**
- Wellness & mindfulness → Kyudo, Zen breathing, tea ceremony
- Quiet luxury → private access, small group, no crowds
- Cultural depth → real masters, real traditions, not staged tourism
- Longevity / inner calm → the day is built around *seitai* (整える), aligning body and mind

## Detailed Flow / 詳細

### 09:00 — Atsuta Shrine / 熱田神宮
A private, early-morning visit before the crowds. Atsuta enshrines the
*Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi*, one of the three Imperial Regalia of Japan. We guide the
guest through proper purification (*temizu*), respectful prayer, and the meaning
of the shrine — not as sightseeing, but as a quiet act of arrival and gratitude.

朝一番、人の少ない時間に正式参拝。手水・作法・神話の背景を丁寧に案内。

### 10:30 — Kyudo / 弓道
The heart of the day. A private session with an authentic kyudo practitioner in
a quiet dojo. The guest learns posture, breathing, *tanden* (center of gravity),
and the philosophy: **"The bow reflects the condition of the person who holds it."**
This is not about hitting the target — it is about meeting oneself.

静かな道場で本格的な弓道体験。姿勢・呼吸・丹田・精神性を学ぶ。
「弓は引く人の状態を映す」。的中ではなく、自分自身と向き合う体験。

### 12:30 — Hitsumabushi / ひつまぶし
Lunch in a private room at a refined Nagoya eel restaurant. Hitsumabushi is the
city's signature dish — eaten three ways, the last with dashi broth. A calm,
elegant meal, with the story behind the food.

個室でひつまぶし。名古屋名物を三つの食べ方で。食の背景も解説。

### 14:00 — Tea Ceremony / 茶道
In a traditional tea room, the guest experiences the quiet ritual of *chanoyu* —
the philosophy of *ichigo ichie* ("one time, one meeting"), the beauty of
imperfection (*wabi-sabi*), and full presence. A wellness experience in the
deepest sense.

茶室で茶道体験。一期一会・侘び寂び・「今ここ」に在る豊かさを体感。

### 15:30 — Nagoya Castle & Hommaru Palace / 名古屋城・本丸御殿
The reconstructed Hommaru Palace — gold leaf, cypress, and the artistry of the
samurai elite. Here the guest sees the *power* and *aesthetics* of the samurai
world, completing the arc from the sacred (shrine) to the inner (kyudo/tea) to
the worldly (castle).

本丸御殿を中心に武家文化を体感。聖（神社）→内面（弓・茶）→現世（城）の流れ。

### 19:00 — Optional Dinner / 任意：夕食
Sushi omakase or seasonal kaiseki in a private setting, to close the day.

任意で寿司おまかせ・会席を個室で。

# Online Meeting Script — Lyn Muto (Lynvé) × Kensuke Ueoka (BUSHIDO JAPAN)

> **Date / 日時**: June 25, 2026, 10:00 (online)
> **Goal / 目標**: Build trust, share the model plan, and agree on a concrete next step.
> **Tone / トーン**: Warm, calm, confident. Not a sales pitch. A meeting of two
> founders with shared values. 売り込みではなく、価値観を共有する対話。

**How to use this script / 使い方**: This is a *guide*, not a word-for-word reading.
Speak naturally. The Japanese notes (➡) tell you the intent of each part so you can
improvise. Aim to *listen more than you talk* — especially in Part 3.

---

## Part 0 — Before the call / 事前準備 (5 min)
- Open the model plan (`nagoya-1day-vip-model-plan.md`) ready to screen-share.
- Have 1–2 photos ready (kyudo, Atsuta Shrine, tea ceremony) if possible.
- Quiet room, good light, camera at eye level. Smile when you join.
- Have water nearby. Breathe. You are the host of this world. / あなたが日本側の主。落ち着いて。

---

## Part 1 — Opening & rapport / 挨拶と関係づくり (3–5 min)
➡ 目的：温かく入る。緊張をほぐす。人として繋がる。

> "Hi Lyn, it's so nice to finally meet you. Thank you for reaching out — your
> message really resonated with me."

> "Before we talk business, I'd love to know a little about you. What made you
> start Lynvé, and what drew you to Japan?"

➡ ここでまず**相手に話してもらう**。Lynの想い・背景を引き出す。メモを取る。
➡ 共通点が出たら拾う：「That's exactly how I feel too.」

---

## Part 2 — Introduce BUSHIDO JAPAN / 自己紹介 (3–4 min)
➡ 目的：ストーリーで語る。スペックではなく「なぜ」を伝える。

> "Let me share a little about BUSHIDO JAPAN. We create private cultural
> experiences rooted in the Japanese spirit — Kyudo (archery), tea ceremony,
> Zen, calligraphy, shrine visits, samurai culture, and local cuisine."

> "But for us, this is not tourism. Our philosophy is simple: *BUSHIDO is not
> tourism — it is transformation.* The bow is not just a tool; it reflects the
> condition of the person who holds it. We work with real masters and real
> traditions, and we protect the essence rather than turning it into a show."

> "When I read about Lynvé — wellness, longevity, quiet luxury, the bridge
> between Switzerland and Japan — I felt we see the world the same way."

➡ キーフレーズ：*"transformation, not tourism"* と *"the bow reflects the soul"*。
➡ ここで自然に「価値観が近い」を伝える。

---

## Part 3 — Discovery: ask & listen / ヒアリング (10–15 min) ★最重要
➡ 目的：聞く。Lynのビジネスと顧客を理解する。ここが一番大事。話す:聞く = 3:7。

Ask these one at a time, and let her talk:

1. **Clients / 顧客層**
   > "Could you tell me about your clients? Are they high-net-worth individuals,
   > wellness travelers, corporate VIPs, families, couples?"

2. **What they want in Japan / 日本で求める体験**
   > "When your clients think about Japan, what are they looking for? Is it mostly
   > Tokyo and Kyoto, or would they be open to places like Nagoya and the regions?"

3. **How you work / 送客の形**
   > "How do you usually work with partners on the ground? Do you refer clients to
   > a local partner, or do you prefer to co-create and package experiences together?"

4. **Commercial model / 手数料・条件**
   > "And on the commercial side — how do you normally structure things? Referral
   > fee, net rates with your own markup, or a shared package? I want to make sure
   > it works well for both of us."

5. **First step / 最初の一歩**
   > "If we were to start with one signature experience together, what would feel
   > right for your clients?"

➡ 相手の答えを要約して返す：「So if I understand you correctly, your clients value...」
➡ 数字や条件は**この場で即決しない**。「Let me put together a proposal based on this.」

---

## Part 4 — Show the model plan / モデルプラン提示 (5–7 min)
➡ 目的：世界観を見せる。「あなたの顧客に、これを日本側で実現できます」。

> "Let me show you something I prepared — a sample of how we think. This is a
> one-day VIP cultural experience in Nagoya. It's just a model — everything is
> fully customizable — but it shows our approach."

Walk through the arc (share screen):
- "We begin at **Atsuta Shrine** — one of Japan's three most sacred shrines,
  privately, before the crowds. A quiet arrival."
- "Then **Kyudo** with a master — posture, breathing, presence. This is the
  wellness heart of the day."
- "A refined local lunch — **hitsumabushi**, Nagoya's signature dish, in a private room."
- "A **tea ceremony** — stillness, *ichigo ichie*, one time, one meeting."
- "And **Nagoya Castle** — the world of the samurai."

> "We chose Nagoya deliberately — it's the heart of samurai history, but far less
> crowded than Tokyo or Kyoto. Perfect for quiet luxury. And we can extend it into
> Kyoto, Ise, or Mt. Koya for a multi-day journey."

➡ 反応を見る。「Which part feels most exciting for your clients?」と聞く。

---

## Part 5 — Collaboration & next step / 協業と次の一歩 (5 min)
➡ 目的：具体的な次のアクションを1つ決める。

> "I'd love to explore building something together — your network in Switzerland
> and Europe, and our cultural experiences and trusted local connections in Japan."

> "Here's what I suggest as a first step: based on what you've told me today, I'll
> put together a tailored proposal for one signature experience — for example, this
> Nagoya VIP day — with clear options on pricing and how we'd work together. Would
> that be helpful?"

➡ 次の約束を取る：「Shall we set a follow-up call once you've seen the proposal?」
➡ 期限を言う：「I can send it to you within [X] days.」

---

## Part 6 — Close / 締め (2 min)
> "Thank you so much, Lyn. I really enjoyed this — I think we share the same
> values, and I'm excited about what we can build together between Switzerland
> and Japan."

> "I'll follow up with the proposal and a short summary of today. Have a wonderful day."

➡ 笑顔で終わる。当日中に「お礼＋要約＋次のステップ」のメールを送る。

## Useful phrases / 使える表現
| Situation | Phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Agreeing / 共感 | "That resonates with me." / "I feel exactly the same." |
| Buying time on numbers / 条件を即決しない | "Let me put that together properly and send it to you." |
| Reflecting back / 要約 | "So if I understand correctly, what matters most to you is…" |
| Showing depth / 本質 | "For us, this is not tourism — it's transformation." |
| Inviting her in / 巻き込む | "How do you imagine your clients experiencing this?" |
| Closing a step / 次の一歩 | "What would be a good next step for us?" |

---

## Quick self-reminders / 自分への注意
- 🎧 **Listen more than you speak.** Especially Part 3. / 聞く方が多く。
- 🐢 **Slow down.** Calm = confidence = quiet luxury. / ゆっくり話す。
- ❌ **Don't oversell.** You are offering a world, not begging for a deal. / 売り込まない。
- 💰 **Don't commit to numbers live.** Promise a proposal instead. / 数字は即決しない。
- ✅ **End with ONE clear next step.** / 必ず次の一歩を1つ決める。

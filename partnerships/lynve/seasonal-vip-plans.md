## 🌸 Spring — Cherry Blossoms / 桜
**If it's sakura → here.** / サクラならここ。

**Flagship: Kyoto + Yoshino (Nara) / 京都＋吉野**
- **Daigo-ji, Kyoto** — site of Toyotomi Hideyoshi's legendary 1598 cherry-blossom
  party (*Daigo no Hanami*). A samurai story written in petals. / 醍醐寺＝秀吉の「醍醐の花見」の地。
- **Mt. Yoshino, Nara** — thousands of cherry trees blooming up the mountainside,
  one of Japan's most sacred and breathtaking hanami sites. / 吉野山＝日本随一の桜の聖地。
- **Private night-viewing** (*yozakura*) with tea, and a quiet morning temple visit.

**Local (from Nagoya)**: cherry blossoms at **Nagoya Castle** and **Inuyama Castle**
(national-treasure keep framed by sakura). / 名古屋城・犬山城の桜。

**BUSHIDO overlay**: tea ceremony under the blossoms, calligraphy of a spring poem,
the *mono-no-aware* of falling petals — beauty because it does not last.
桜の下での茶道・書道、「もののあはれ」の精神。

## 🍁 Autumn — Autumn Leaves / 紅葉
**If it's koyo → here.** / 紅葉ならここ。

**Flagship: Kyoto temples / 京都の寺社**
- **Eikan-do & Tofuku-ji** — among Japan's most exquisite maple temples. / 永観堂・東福寺。
- **Private early-morning access** before the crowds; a quiet, meditative walk.
- Optional **Mt. Koya (Koyasan)** — autumn among ancient cedars and temple lodging
  (*shukubo*), Zen and stillness. / 高野山＝宿坊・禅・静寂。

**Local (from Nagoya)**: **Korankei (香嵐渓)** in Aichi — one of central Japan's most
famous maple valleys, ~4,000 maple trees along the river. / 香嵐渓＝東海屈指の紅葉名所。

**BUSHIDO overlay**: Zen meditation among the maples, kyudo with autumn light, a
seasonal *kaiseki* of autumn flavors. 紅葉の中での座禅・弓道、秋の会席。

## 🏖️ Summer — Beach & Longevity / ビーチ
**If it's beach → here.** / ビーチならここ。

**Flagship: Okinawa / 沖縄**
- **Okinawa is a recognized "Blue Zone"** — one of the world's regions famous for
  longevity. A perfect match for Lynvé's wellness & longevity philosophy.
  沖縄は世界的な**ブルーゾーン（長寿地域）**。Lynvéの「ロンジェビティ」と完全一致。
- **Ishigaki & Iriomote** — turquoise water, private beaches, mangrove and reef,
  far from the crowds. / 石垣・西表＝静かなプライベートビーチ。
- Wellness focus: longevity cuisine, mindfulness by the sea, marine activities.

**Local (from Nagoya / central)**: **Ise-Shima** — sacred **Ise Grand Shrine** plus
the quiet coves and pearl culture of Shima. / 伊勢志摩＝伊勢神宮＋静かな海岸。

**BUSHIDO overlay**: morning kyudo or breathing practice on the shore, a longevity
*washoku* meal, and the spiritual depth of Ise. 海辺での弓道・呼吸法、長寿和食、伊勢の精神性。

## ⛷️ Winter — Snow & Powder / スキー
**If it's ski → here.** / スキーならここ。

**Flagship: Niseko, Hokkaido / ニセコ（北海道）**
- **World-renowned powder snow** and Japan's premier luxury ski destination, with
  international-standard private chalets and fine dining. / 世界的パウダースノー、ラグジュアリーの定番。
- Private guides, onsen under the snow, and après-ski in refined comfort.

**Alternatives / 代替案**:
- **Hakuba (Nagano)** — Olympic slopes plus deep mountain culture. / 白馬。
- **Gero Onsen (Gifu)** — from a Nagoya base, one of Japan's top three hot springs,
  snow + healing waters + culture (less skiing, more wellness). / 下呂温泉＝名古屋から好アクセス。

**BUSHIDO overlay**: the discipline of cold and stillness, a snow-side tea ceremony,
calligraphy by the fire, deep-soak onsen wellness. 雪と静寂の鍛錬、雪見茶道、温泉ウェルネス。

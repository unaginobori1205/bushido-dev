## Slide 3 — The Problem (Demand side)

Travelers want transformation, not photos — but authentic culture is:

- **Fragmented** — no single place to find real masters
- **Unbookable** — phone-only, Japanese-only, no online payment
- **Untranslated** — language *and* cultural meaning are lost

---

## Slide 4 — The Problem (Supply side)

Japan's masters are world-class in depth, weak in distribution:

- No English, no online booking, no payment, no scheduling
- No quality / authenticity signal for foreign buyers
- The result: **authentic value that cannot be distributed**

---

## Slide 5 — The Solution

**BUSHIDO AI** — an AI cultural intelligence layer that assembles a complete,
trusted journey from one input: *who you are and why you're coming.*

- Inputs: interests · purpose · budget · location · learning goals
- Outputs: masters + interpreters + itinerary + transport + lodging, as one booking

---

## Slide 6 — How It Works (the tech)

The defensible core is **data + matching**, not the experiences themselves:

1. **Cultural Intelligence Engine** — matches visitor intent to experiences
2. **Trust & Verification Layer** — vets masters; community-scored authenticity
3. **Real-time Cultural Translation** — AI interpretation of meaning, not just words
4. **Itinerary Orchestration** — bundles logistics into one trusted flow

- *Visual:* simple architecture diagram (Visitor → Engine → Trust Layer → Booking).

- Regional Japan needs distribution; travelers need trusted access — the gap is open

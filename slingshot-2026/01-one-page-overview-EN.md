### The Problem

Japan welcomes tens of millions of inbound visitors each year, yet most travelers
only ever reach surface-level sightseeing. The country's deepest assets — *kyudo*
(Japanese archery), tea ceremony, calligraphy, Zen, shrine and samurai culture,
and the living masters who carry these traditions — remain **fragmented, hard to
discover, hard to book, and hard to understand** across language and cultural barriers.

On the supply side, regional masters and cultural sites are world-class in depth but
weak in English, online booking, payment, scheduling, and quality assurance. The
authentic experience economy is real but **non-digital and non-distributable** today.

### The Solution

**BUSHIDO AI** is an AI-powered cultural intelligence platform that connects global
travelers, schools, corporates, and premium clients with authentic Japanese cultural
experiences. Our engine personalizes a cultural journey from the visitor's
**interests, purpose, budget, location, and learning goals**, then assembles a single
trusted experience layer from:

- **Verified local masters** (vetted practitioners, not actors)
- **Interpreters & cultural translation** (meaning, not just words)
- **Itinerary design, premium transport & lodging**
- **Trust-based community data** that scores authenticity and quality

We turn Japan's non-digital cultural value into a distributable, bookable,
quality-assured product — a **cultural experience OS**.

### Why Now / Why Us

- Record post-pandemic inbound demand and a structural shift toward **experiential,
  high-value travel** over mass sightseeing.
- AI now makes deep personalization and real-time cultural translation viable at scale.
- **Founder–market fit:** the founder is a practicing *kyudo* and *bushido* educator

### English

> **Who I am**
>
> My name is Kensuke Ueoka. I was born and raised in Japan, and I have dedicated my life to one question:
> *What is the true spirit of Japan — and how can I share it with the world?*
>
> For me, the answer is not found in souvenirs or photo spots. It lives in **Bushido** — the way of the samurai — and in disciplines like **Kyudo**, Japanese archery, where every breath, every step, and every posture becomes a form of self-cultivation.
>
> In Kyudo, hitting the target is not the goal. The goal is to refine the self. A bamboo bow is alive — it changes with the temperature, the humidity, the time of day. To draw it well, you must first put your own heart in order.
>
> This is the philosophy I bring to everything I do:
> - As founder of **BUSHIDO JAPAN**, I design authentic experiences that turn cultural tourism into genuine transformation.
> - As an organizer of the **Nagoya Inbound Summit**, I work to make Nagoya a gateway where the world meets the real Japan.
>
> I believe travel can change a person. Not by what they see — but by what they become.
>
> If this resonates with you, I would be honored to call you a friend. Welcome.

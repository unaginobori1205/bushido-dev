Salaam Japan

Muslim-friendly Central Japan

We are not halal-certified, and we will not hand you a certificate we do not have.

What we have instead: every venue is checked for the specific group, before the trip, in Japanese — and the people who check are Muslims living in Nagoya who eat there themselves. Not a list copied off a website. This work is done with Halal Navi.

For each venue we tell you exactly what it is — Muslim-owned, verified by our Muslim partners, or simply no pork and no alcohol in the kitchen. You decide what your client can accept.

Prayer is written into the schedule, five times a day, with somewhere to wash. Nobody is ever asked to take part in a religious act at a shrine or temple.

Tokyo and Osaka have established halal certification. Nagoya does not yet. We would rather you heard that from us.

Archery and horsemanship are not merely permitted in Islam — they are encouraged. Yabusame is both at once.

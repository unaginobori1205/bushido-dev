We do not buy our experiences from a wholesaler. I teach the archery. The calligraphy master is someone I train with.

I chair the Nagoya Inbound Summit — 100+ operators across Aichi, Gifu and Mie — and we are a member of the Nagoya Convention & Visitors Bureau. For transport we work with Shachi Bus.

It sits on the Tokyo–Kyoto line. Your clients lose no travel time — and gain a day of Japan that is not crowded, at lower cost than Kyoto in peak season.

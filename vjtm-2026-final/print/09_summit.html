What it is

A working network of the people who actually deliver inbound travel in Central Japan — hotels (Hilton, ESPACIO Nagoya Castle), the Nagoya Convention & Visitors Bureau, city tourism officials, prefectural assembly members, restaurants, craft studios and experience providers. We meet regularly for one purpose: to turn Nagoya from a place trains pass through into a place people stop.

BUSHIDO LLC, which organises the Summit, is itself a member of the Nagoya Convention & Visitors Bureau.

Why this matters to your agency

  • One introduction, many partners. Reach vetted hotels, restaurants, craft masters and experience hosts across Aichi, Gifu, Mie and the wider Chubu region through a single contact — mine.
  • Nagoya is not a detour. It sits on the Tokyo–Kyoto line. Your clients lose no travel time. They gain a day of Japan that is not crowded, at a lower cost than Kyoto in peak season.
  • 2027 is already moving. The JCI World Congress brings thousands of delegates to Nagoya in November 2027. Agencies building Chubu programmes now will be the ones with inventory when it arrives.

What we can arrange for your clients

Culture

Private kyudo taught by a 4th-dan master, yabusame horseback archery, calligraphy, tea ceremony, Arimatsu indigo, pottery, washi.

Gastronomy of Tokai

Miso breweries, sake cellars, unagi, and hotel dining led by a chef formerly of SUGALABO (La Liste world No. 1).

SALAAM JAPAN

Our Muslim-friendly programme. Dining verified venue by venue rather than copied from a list, prayer space mapped in advance, and a strict principle that no one is ever asked to take part in a religious act.

B2B support

Net rates, sample itineraries from half-day stopovers to three days, FAM trip assistance, groups from 1 to 100.

## C. 全商品共通の条件（英語・そのまま読み上げ可）

> **Minimum numbers** Private programmes run from two guests. There is no single-guest supplement — we simply quote for two.
>
> **Maximum per day** Kyudo is capped by what one instructor can teach in a day, not by commercial choice. Larger parties are run as rotations, with the waiting group sent to a second experience and swapped over.
>
> **English** Every programme is delivered in English. Chinese interpretation is arranged on request. Other languages on request.
>
> **Rain** All indoor programmes run regardless of weather. Yabusame and sea fishing are the exceptions — we postpone rather than run them badly, and we tell you 24 hours ahead.
>
> **Lead time** 14 days standard. 30 days for groups over 20. 60 days for yabusame.
>
> **Cancellation** Free to 8 days before. 30% at 7–4 days. 50% at 3–1 days. 100% on the day. Where a venue, a master, ingredients or a vehicle has been committed in advance, those costs are passed at actual cost — and we tell you which ones before you confirm, not after.
>
> **Commission** 15%, shown as a net rate next to retail on every quote. Items marked *at cost* carry no commission: hotels, admissions, yabusame, the kyudo camp. We would rather tell you that now than have you find it in the invoice.
>
> **Booking** E-mail. Confirmation and invoice within 24 hours. Card payment link available.
>
> **Customisation** Everything here is a component. Take one, take three days, or tell us the shape of the day you need and we build it. Nothing in this catalogue has to be bought whole.

### AI生成画像の扱い（2026-09-24 上岡さん判断で確定）

**方針：仮画像として入れる。実写が撮れ次第、差し替える。**

意見は一度申し上げましたが、判断は理解しました。実務上そうするしかない箇所があります。
そのうえで**差し替え漏れを防ぐための運用だけ**決めておきます。

| ルール | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| **1. 弓道には使わない** | 実写が4枚ある。**主力商品に仮画像を混ぜない**。ここだけは本物で通す |
| **2. 人の顔を作らない** | 「この人が講師です」と誤解される。**手元・道具・背景・場の空気**にとどめる |
| **3. 実在の店舗・施設を模さない** | 特定の店だと誤認されると、その店とこちらの両方が困る |
| **4. 差し替えリストを持つ** | 下表。**10月中に実写へ差し替える**。放置すると1年残る |
| **5. QR先のLPと紙で同じ画像を使う** | 別々の画像だと、どちらが本物か分からなくなる |
